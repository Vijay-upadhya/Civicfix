Privacy policy

Version 2026-10 · Demo project

CivicFix stores the details you enter (name, email, optional phone and ward, complaint text, location and photos) so that complaints can be routed and tracked.

  • Public complaints show a title, description, location and photos, but not your contact details.
  • Officers and administrators can see who submitted a complaint.
  • We record that you accepted this policy, with the time and IP address.
  • Do not upload photos of people or personal documents.

If you sign in with Facebook or X we receive your name, email (if shared) and profile id from that service, and nothing else. See how to delete your data.

Location: we only use your device location, nearby-issue search and address lookup after you allow it. Address lookup sends coordinates to the map provider (Google Maps, or OpenStreetMap's Nominatim when Google is not configured), and the map is loaded from that provider, which can see your IP address. You can withdraw permission on your profile page.

This is a demonstration application. Do not submit real personal data.
