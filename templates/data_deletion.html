Data deletion instructions

You can ask us to delete your CivicFix account and the personal data linked to it (name, email, phone, ward and your complaints) at any time.

  1. Email contact@civicfix.example from the address on your account (or the Facebook/X account you used to sign in), with the subject Delete my CivicFix data.
  2. We will confirm the request and remove the data. Removing a Facebook or X login from your CivicFix account does not delete anything on those services.
  3. You can also remove CivicFix from your Facebook settings (Settings → Apps and Websites) to stop it signing you in.

This is a demonstration project; replace the contact address with a real one before going live.
