About CivicFix

Report it. Track it. Fix it.

CivicFix is a civic-tech portfolio project that shows how residents, department officers and administrators can work through the full life cycle of a neighbourhood complaint: reporting, routing, duplicate detection, priority scoring, SLA monitoring and analytics.

It is not connected to any real government system and contains no real citizen data. All complaints, accounts and statistics shown in the demo are synthetic.

Built with Django, Django REST Framework, Leaflet, Chart.js and scikit-learn.
